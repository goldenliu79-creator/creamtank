FAQ — food-grade N₂O cream chargers (B2B)

Buyer-phrased answers for wholesale E942 cream chargers: MOQ, food-grade use, EU / UK fulfilment countries, documents, and how to inquire.

What are food-grade N₂O cream chargers (E942) used for?

Food-grade N₂O cream chargers are large-format cylinders of nitrous oxide authorised as food additive E942, used as a whipped-cream / food propellant in professional foodservice, bakery and food-processing channels. CreamTank supplies them for verified B2B buyers only — not for recreational use.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale?

Minimum order is 1 pallet. We do not fulfil loose retail or small-parcel orders. Quotes cover product plus dangerous-goods freight priced to your destination.

Which EU and UK countries can you fulfil from?

We offer EU warehouse fulfilment through a network in France, Germany and Spain, plus UK warehouse fulfilment for selected SKUs such as FastGas ~670g. Exact routing and stock are confirmed on inquiry.

How fast can EU pallet orders be delivered?

EU warehouse stock is ready for professional B2B pallet orders, with typical delivery within 7 days depending on destination and DG carrier scheduling. Your quote states the expected lead time.

Who can buy CreamTank food-grade cream chargers?

Verified business customers with a lawful foodservice, bakery, food-processing or industrial end use. Recreational-use requests are declined. Buyer credentials and intended use are part of every wholesale conversation.

Do you provide certificates, SDS and compliance documents?

For qualified B2B buyers, CreamTank can share cylinder conformity documents under TPED (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118) on request against the SKU you inquire. Products are offered as food-grade wholesale cream chargers; an E942 COA is not claimed as part of the standard public pack. Ask for the current document list with your inquiry.
