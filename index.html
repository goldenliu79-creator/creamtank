What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Minimum order is 1 pallet. We do not fulfil loose retail or small-parcel orders. Default EU pallet load is 504 pcs (see pallet quantity). Quotes cover product plus dangerous-goods freight priced to your destination.

Do you send samples?

No samples. Wholesale starts at 1 pallet MOQ only — we do not ship loose retail parcels or sample packs (heavy DG product · UN1070). Learn more — no samples.

Who can buy CreamTank food-grade cream chargers?

Verified business customers with a lawful foodservice, bakery, food-processing or industrial end use. Recreational-use requests are declined. Buyer credentials and intended use are part of every wholesale conversation. Learn more — who can buy · EU B2B buying.

Do you provide TPED 0036 and other documents?

For qualified B2B buyers, CreamTank can share cylinder conformity documents under TPED (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118) on request against the SKU you inquire. Products are offered as food-grade wholesale cream chargers; an E942 COA is not claimed as part of the standard public pack. Ask for the current document list with your inquiry.
