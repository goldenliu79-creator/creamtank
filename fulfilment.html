Dangerous goods shipping

Cream charger cylinders ship as UN 1070 · Class 2.2 / 5.1 with approved dangerous-goods capable carriers (UN1070 shipping answer). Ordinary postal gas routes are not used. Lead times follow DG carrier scheduling for your destination.

Documents for qualified B2B buyers

TPED cylinder conformity

TPED (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118 / 2010/35/EU) cylinder conformity documents are available on request to qualified B2B buyers against the SKU you inquire. Hub: documents · deep answer: TPED 0036.

Food-grade / E942 positioning

Products are offered as food-grade / E942 whipped-cream propellant for professional channels (pack marking / product positioning). An E942 certificate of analysis (COA) is not claimed as part of the standard public pack.

Buyer due diligence

We sell only to verified business customers for lawful food or industrial use. Recreational-use requests are declined.

Fulfilment FAQ

Which warehouses fulfil EU pallet orders?

France (main), Germany and Spain. We route from the nearest warehouse that can cover your order from available stock. UK warehouse fulfilment is an option for selected SKUs and UK addresses.

Is EU stock ready for delivery within 7 days?

Yes for eligible B2B pallet destinations: EU warehouse stock is ready, and after payment the working target is delivery within 7 days (truck booking typically 1–3 days; transit typically 4–48h by distance). Your quote states expected timing.

Do you publish freight rates on this page?

No. Dangerous-goods freight is calculated to your delivery address. Inquire for a wholesale quote that includes product and freight assumptions.

Can German buyers be supplied if Germany warehouse stock is not ready?

Yes. German delivery addresses can be fulfilled from other EU warehouses (for example France or Spain) when that is nearer, faster, or the available option. Routing is confirmed on inquiry — we do not separately promise DE warehouse stock ready. Learn more — German delivery.
