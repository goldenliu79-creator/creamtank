Nearest-warehouse policy

We select fulfilment based on where the goods must arrive — not a single fixed origin for every EU order.

EU (FR · DE · ES)

For EU delivery addresses we prefer the nearest warehouse among France, Germany and Spain that can cover the SKU and pallet quantity on your quote.

UK

UK destinations are fulfilled from the UK warehouse for selected SKUs such as FastGas ~670g. Availability and DG freight are confirmed per inquiry.

Germany flexibility

German buyers can be supplied from other EU warehouses when that is nearer, faster, or the only stocked option. We confirm routing on the quote — we do not wait solely on one warehouse if another can serve you.

Lead time after payment

Truck booking

After payment clears, dangerous-goods truck booking typically takes 1–3 days.

Transit

Once on the road, transit is typically 4–48 hours depending on distance and DG carrier scheduling.

Quotes are issued as delivered (DDP / door-to-door style) discussions for professional B2B buyers. Exact timing for your postcode is stated on the quote. No public list prices on this site.

Order & shipping rules

Pallet MOQ

Minimum order is 1 pallet. We do not fulfil loose retail or small-parcel orders. EU pallets typically default to about 504 pcs (a lower ~432 pcs load may apply for smaller demand).

UN1070 DG freight

Shipments move as UN 1070 · Class 2.2 with 5.1 with approved DG-capable carriers. Freight is priced to your delivery address on inquiry.

No samples

Cylinders are heavy large-format wholesale units. CreamTank does not ship samples — quotes start at pallet MOQ for verified B2B buyers.

Fulfilment FAQ

How is the warehouse chosen?

By delivery address, prioritizing the nearest warehouse with available stock among FR / DE / ES for EU, and the UK warehouse for UK destinations. Alternate routing is confirmed if stock is short.

Documents on request

For qualified B2B buyers, cylinder conformity under TPED (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118) can be shared against the SKU you inquire. Ask with your quote request.
