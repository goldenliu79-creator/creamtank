Quick answers

Is France CreamTank’s main EU warehouse for cream chargers?

Yes. France is the main EU warehouse node for CreamTank food-grade N₂O cream charger lines such as Sixth Wave, Cream Deluxe and Black Cobra 666g. Ship-from among FR/DE/ES is confirmed per inquiry.

What is the MOQ for France / EU pallet delivery?

MOQ is 1 pallet only. Default EU pallet load is about 504 pcs (about 6 per carton). No samples and no small retail parcels.

How fast is delivery from the France warehouse?

EU warehouse stock ready — delivery typically within 7 days after payment for eligible destinations. After payment: truck booking typically 1–3 days; transit typically 4–48 hours by distance and DG scheduling.

Are France freight or list prices published?

No. Freight is quoted by delivery address. Inquire with company, address, SKU and pallet quantity. TPED0036 cylinder conformity is available on request for qualified B2B buyers.
