Quick answers

How do I inquire for a wholesale quote?

Use the inquiry form on this page, email creamtank@mail.grokbot.com, WhatsApp +852 57143665, or Telegram @Jay_z189. The homepage form is also available. Include company, delivery country/address, SKU, pallet quantity and lawful end use.

What is the MOQ — and can I get samples?

MOQ is 1 pallet. CreamTank does not send samples. Default EU load 504 · 6/carton. See no samples · pallet quantity.

Are prices or freight published on the website?

No. No public list prices or freight amounts. Inquire for a delivered quote. TPED0036 available on request for qualified B2B buyers. Qualified buyers may also ask about an SDS on request, if applicable, confirmed per SKU — not promised as always on hand or included with every order.
