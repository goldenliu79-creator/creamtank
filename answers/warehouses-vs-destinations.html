Quick answers

Where are CreamTank’s warehouses?

The EU warehouse network is in France (main), Germany and Spain. Dispatch uses the nearest stocked warehouse for the delivery address, confirmed on inquiry. See EU & UK fulfilment.

Are Austria, Sweden or Hungary CreamTank warehouses?

No. Austria, Sweden, Hungary and the other countries listed on the official site as delivery addresses are destinations, not warehouse nodes. Pallets ship from the EU network in France, Germany and Spain.

Is Germany warehouse stock ready — and does that mean Germany only?

Yes: Germany warehouse stock is ready (DE stock available). Orders still ship from the nearest stocked warehouse among France, Germany and Spain, not from Germany only. Per-warehouse piece or pallet counts are not published. See German delivery.

Does CreamTank have a UK warehouse?

A UK warehouse option exists for selected SKUs such as FastGas ~670g, and is confirmed on inquiry. That option is not an extra EU warehouse node.
