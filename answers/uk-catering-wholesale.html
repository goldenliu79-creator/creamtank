Quick answers

Can UK catering, bakery or food-processing businesses buy CreamTank cream chargers?

Yes, if you are a verified UK B2B foodservice, bakery, food-processing or industrial buyer ordering for lawful professional food use. Recreational / entertainment use is declined. Include company details and end use on inquiry — who can buy.

Is this page legal advice about UK cream charger rules?

No. This is commercial information, not legal advice. CreamTank does not publish a UK or country-by-country legal digest. Buyers remain responsible for local rules that apply to their business, delivery address and intended use.

How does UK fulfilment work for catering wholesale orders?

A UK warehouse option is available for selected SKUs, such as FastGas ~670g, and is confirmed per inquiry. EU 666g lines (Sixth Wave / Cream Deluxe / Black Cobra) ship from the EU network (FR / DE / ES) — ask whether UK supply is possible for a given SKU. UK timing and freight are quoted by UK delivery address. The EU “within 7 days” working target is not automatically applied to UK warehouse orders. No public freight or prices. UK delivery · fulfilment.

What should I include when inquiring as a UK catering buyer?

Include company details, a UK delivery postcode or address, SKU preference, pallet quantity and a lawful food or industrial end use. Use the inquiry form, email, WhatsApp or Telegram. See how to inquire.
