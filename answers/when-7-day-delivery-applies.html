When CreamTank’s “delivery typically within 7 days” wording applies

The public wording “EU warehouse stock ready — delivery typically within 7 days” is a working target for eligible pallet orders served from CreamTank’s EU warehouse network in France (main), Germany and Spain, after payment. It is not a calendar guarantee, and it does not apply automatically to every CreamTank quote. After payment, the usual EU-network sequence is truck booking typically 1–3 days, then transit typically 4–48 hours by distance and dangerous-goods carrier scheduling. Exact timing is confirmed on your quote.

Quick answers

When does “delivery typically within 7 days” apply?

It is a working target for eligible verified B2B pallet orders served from CreamTank’s EU warehouse network in France (main), Germany and Spain to eligible EU destinations, after payment. Exact timing is confirmed on your quote. See lead time for the sequence after payment.

Does it apply to UK warehouse orders?

Not automatically. The EU “typically within 7 days” wording is not automatically applied to UK warehouse orders (for example selected SKUs such as FastGas ~670g). Timing is quoted for the UK delivery address — UK delivery.

Does it apply to Switzerland?

Not automatically. Switzerland sits outside the EU customs area. Timing and delivery terms for a Swiss address are confirmed on inquiry. This page does not invent customs, duty, tax or border-crossing rules — Switzerland delivery.

Is “typically within 7 days” a guaranteed delivery date?

No. It is a working target, not a guaranteed calendar date. For eligible EU-network orders, the usual sequence after payment is truck booking typically 1–3 days, then transit typically 4–48 hours by distance and dangerous-goods scheduling. Your quote states the expected timing.

Are freight amounts published with the 7-day wording?

No. Freight is quoted by delivery address. There are no public freight amounts or list prices. TPED0036 cylinder conformity is available on request for qualified B2B buyers.
