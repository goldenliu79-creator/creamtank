How cream chargers ship: UN1070 · Class 2.2 / 5.1

Nitrous oxide (N₂O) cream charger cylinders are transported as dangerous goods UN1070, Class 2.2 with subsidiary 5.1 (oxidiser). CreamTank moves pallet orders only with approved dangerous-goods capable carriers — ordinary postal gas routes are not used.

Quick answers

What is the UN number for nitrous oxide cream chargers?

UN1070 (nitrous oxide), Class 2.2 with subsidiary hazard 5.1 (oxidiser).

Can cream charger cylinders be sent by post or parcel courier?

CreamTank does not use ordinary postal gas routes. Pallets move with approved dangerous-goods capable carriers, which is why the minimum order is 1 pallet and no samples are shipped.

Does DG shipping change the delivery time?

Timing follows DG carrier scheduling. For EU stock: after payment, truck booking typically 1–3 days and transit typically 4–48 hours — delivery typically within 7 days.

Which documents are available for the cylinders?

TPED cylinder conformity (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118) is available on request for qualified B2B buyers against the SKU inquired.
