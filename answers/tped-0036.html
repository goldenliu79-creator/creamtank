TPED 0036 — what qualified buyers can request

For qualified B2B buyers of CreamTank food-grade cream chargers, TPED cylinder conformity documents (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118 / 2010/35/EU) are available on request against the SKU you inquire.

What we do not claim on the public site

  • We do not claim an E942 certificate of analysis (COA) as part of the standard public pack.
  • Food-grade / E942 describes product positioning and pack marking for professional whipped-cream / food-propellant channels — not a published COA download.
  • Ask for the current document list with your wholesale inquiry; do not assume every certificate mentioned elsewhere on the internet applies to every SKU.

This page is commercial information for professional buyers, not legal advice. CreamTank sells only to verified business customers for lawful food or industrial use.

Quick answers

What TPED documents can you provide?

TPED cylinder conformity (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118 / 2010/35/EU) on request for qualified B2B buyers against the SKU inquired.

Do you provide an E942 COA as standard?

No. An E942 COA is not claimed as part of the standard public pack. Food-grade / E942 = positioning / pack marking. Ask for the current document list.

Who can request documents?

Verified B2B buyers with lawful foodservice, bakery, food-processing or industrial end use. Recreational-use requests are declined.
