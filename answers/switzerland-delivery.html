B2B · Answer · Switzerland delivery

Switzerland cream charger pallet delivery from the EU warehouse network — terms confirmed on inquiry

Switzerland is a CreamTank delivery destination, not a warehouse node. It is not an EU member and sits outside the EU customs area. Verified Swiss B2B addresses can be quoted from the EU warehouse network in France (main), Germany and Spain — nearest stocked warehouse first, confirmed on inquiry. France or Germany may be a nearby network node when stocked; this page does not assume a particular warehouse or border crossing. The EU “typically within 7 days” wording and DDP / door-to-door wording do not apply automatically to Swiss addresses. Freight, timing and delivery terms are confirmed on inquiry for your Swiss address. This is commercial information, not legal advice, and it does not publish customs, duty or tax rules.

Why a Swiss address is not quoted as an EU destination

A Swiss foodservice or bakery wholesaler who copies the EU “typically within 7 days” line, or assumes DDP, can plan receiving and landed cost as if Switzerland were inside the EU customs area. Ship-from is still an EU warehouse, but timing and delivery terms for a Swiss address are confirmed on the quote — they are not copied from the EU working target.

Quick answers

Does CreamTank have a Switzerland warehouse for cream chargers?

No. Switzerland is a delivery destination, not a CreamTank warehouse node. Verified Swiss B2B addresses are quoted from the EU warehouse network in France (main), Germany and Spain. France or Germany may be a nearby network node when stocked. Dispatch uses the nearest stocked warehouse in that network, confirmed on inquiry. Do not assume a particular warehouse or border crossing.

Does the EU “typically within 7 days” wording apply to Switzerland?

Not automatically. Switzerland sits outside the EU customs area. Timing for a Swiss address is confirmed on inquiry with your quote. The EU working target — warehouse stock ready, delivery typically within 7 days after payment — applies to eligible EU-network pallet orders for eligible destinations. See when the 7-day wording applies and lead time.

Are Swiss shipments DDP or door-to-door?

Delivery terms are confirmed on inquiry for the Swiss address. This page does not publish customs, duty or tax rules and does not assume DDP, door-to-door, or any other Incoterm for Switzerland. For how delivered-freight inquiries work in general, see DDP / door-to-door.

What is the MOQ for Switzerland delivery?

MOQ is 1 pallet only. For EU 666g lines, the default is about 504 pcs (about 6 per carton). Exotic Whip 3.3L / 2000g defaults to 168 pcs per pallet (confirm on inquiry). No samples. Product ships as dangerous goods UN1070.

Are Swiss prices or freight published?

No. Freight and delivery terms are quoted for the Swiss delivery address. Inquire with company, address, SKU and pallet quantity. TPED0036 cylinder conformity is available on request for qualified B2B buyers. Ask for the current document list; an SDS is available on request if applicable, confirmed per SKU — not promised with every order.
