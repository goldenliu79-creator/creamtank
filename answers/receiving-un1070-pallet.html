CreamTank delivers food-grade N₂O cream chargers as pallet-only dangerous goods UN1070 (Class 2.2 / 5.1) with approved DG-capable carriers — not postal or parcel gas routes. Give a business delivery address that can accept a pallet, and note dock, forklift or tail-lift needs on inquiry.

Quick answers

What should my site be able to receive?

Give a business delivery address that can accept a pallet from a DG-capable carrier. Note dock, forklift or tail-lift needs on inquiry so the booking can be planned.

Can a UN1070 cream charger pallet go by post or parcel courier?

No. CreamTank moves pallet orders only with approved dangerous-goods capable carriers. Ordinary postal or parcel gas routes are not used. See UN1070 DG shipping.

What should I include when I inquire?

Company details, delivery country and address, SKU preference, pallet quantity and lawful food or industrial end use, plus any receiving constraints such as dock, forklift or tail-lift needs. How to inquire.

Which documents are available when a pallet arrives?

TPED0036 cylinder conformity (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118 / 2010/35/EU) is available on request for qualified B2B buyers against the SKU inquired. Ask for the current document list. The public site does not offer a certificate PDF download pack and does not claim a standard E942 COA pack.
