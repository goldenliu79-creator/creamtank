Quick answers

Can German addresses be served from other EU warehouses?

Yes. German delivery addresses can ship from the nearest stocked warehouse among France, Germany and Spain — for example France or Spain when that is the better or available option. Routing is confirmed on inquiry.

Is Germany warehouse stock ready?

Yes. Germany warehouse stock is ready (DE stock available). Orders still ship from the nearest stocked warehouse among France, Germany and Spain. Routing is confirmed on inquiry. This page does not publish live per-warehouse SKU counts.
