Quick answers

Do you send free samples of cream chargers?

No samples. Wholesale starts at 1 pallet MOQ only. Product ships as dangerous goods (UN1070).

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is 1 pallet. Default EU load is 504 pcs · 6 per carton. See pallet quantity.

Can I buy a single cylinder or a few cartons?

No. CreamTank does not fulfil loose retail or small-parcel orders. Pallet wholesale only for verified B2B buyers.
