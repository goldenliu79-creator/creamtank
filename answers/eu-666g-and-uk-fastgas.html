EU 666g lines — ship from the EU network

CreamTank’s publicly listed EU large-format lines are CDL Sixth Wave Edition 666, Cream Deluxe 666g and CDL Black Cobra 666g. They ship from the EU warehouse network in France, Germany and Spain. A further listed EU SKU, Exotic Whip 3.3L / 2000g, is documented on its own product page and is not a 666g line.

The products hub and the pallet-quantity page state a default EU load of about 504 pcs per pallet, 6 pcs per carton, with a special lower-density load of 432 pcs only when logistics require it and it is agreed in writing.

Sources: https://creamtank.com/products.html, pallet quantity, fulfilment (checked 2026-11-25, Asia/Shanghai).

FastGas ~670g — UK warehouse option

FastGas ~670g is a selected SKU with a UK warehouse fulfilment option. FastGas is not described as a generic EU fulfilment item. Availability is confirmed per inquiry. The EU “typically within 7 days” working target is not automatically applied to UK warehouse orders.

The official product short page — not the homepage product card — states about 6 pcs per carton and a default of about 504 pcs per pallet (MOQ 1 pallet; confirm on inquiry). The homepage card for FastGas says pallet quantity is confirmed on inquiry and does not list a piece count there.

Sources: https://creamtank.com/products/fastgas-670.html; homepage FastGas card at https://creamtank.com/ (no piece count on that card); UK catering wholesale.

This page does not restate a full-container piece count, and it does not combine packing figures with the homepage truck/container pallet tiers. For those tiers, see full truck / full container.

What this page does not list

There are no public list prices. Exotic Whip 3.3L / 2000g is a separate listed EU-network SKU (default 168 pcs / pallet; EU warehouse stock ready — delivery within 7 days). It is not one of the EU 666g lines on this page, and it is not the UK warehouse FastGas ~670g SKU. This answers page does not repeat that product’s full specification — use the product page.

Quick answers

Which CreamTank cream chargers ship from the EU warehouse network?

The publicly listed EU large-format lines are CDL Sixth Wave Edition 666, Cream Deluxe 666g and CDL Black Cobra 666g. They ship from France, Germany and Spain. Where does FastGas ~670g ship from, and is it a generic EU item?

FastGas ~670g is a selected SKU with a UK warehouse fulfilment option. It is not described as a generic EU fulfilment item. Availability is confirmed per inquiry. The EU “typically within 7 days” working target is not automatically applied to UK warehouse orders.

How many FastGas units are on a pallet?

The official product short page states about 6 pcs per carton and a default of about 504 pcs per pallet (MOQ 1 pallet; confirm on inquiry). The homepage FastGas card does not show that number — it says pallet quantity is confirmed on inquiry.
