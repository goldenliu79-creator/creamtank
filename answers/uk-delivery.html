Quick answers

Does CreamTank have a UK warehouse for cream chargers?

Yes — a UK warehouse option is available for selected SKUs, such as FastGas ~670g. Availability is confirmed per inquiry.

How many FastGas cream chargers are on a UK pallet?

About 504 pcs per pallet (about 6 per carton). A UK full-container reference is about 12096 pcs. MOQ is 1 pallet; no samples.

Can I order a single box or samples for UK delivery?

No. CreamTank sells B2B pallet wholesale only — MOQ 1 pallet, no samples, no small retail parcels. Product ships as dangerous goods UN1070.

Are UK prices or freight published?

No. Freight is quoted by UK delivery address and the site shows specifications only. Inquire with company, postcode, SKU and pallet quantity. TPED0036 cylinder conformity is available on request for qualified buyers.
