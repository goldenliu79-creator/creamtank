Quick answers

Can I inquire for a full truck or a full container?

Yes. The homepage lists EU shipping tiers on inquiry: pallet freight to your address, or full truck (24 pallets) / full container (26 pallets) delivered pricing. There are no public list prices.

Does this page convert 24 or 26 pallets into a piece count?

No. This page uses only the homepage figures of 24 pallets and 26 pallets. It does not convert those tiers into piece counts and does not publish a price.

What is the minimum order if I ask about a truck or container?

The minimum order remains 1 pallet. CreamTank does not send samples or fulfil loose retail parcels. Quotes are prepared for verified B2B buyers.
