What to send in your inquiry

  • Full delivery address (country, city, postcode) — required for door-to-door freight
  • SKU preference and pallet quantity (MOQ 1 pallet; default EU load 504 pcs)
  • Preferred Incoterms wording if you need DDP / door-to-door vs other terms
  • Lawful foodservice / bakery / food-processing / industrial end use
  • Phone contact (required on the inquiry form)
