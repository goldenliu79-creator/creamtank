Why the distinction matters

In general terms only, professional food-additive use (whipped-cream / food propellant in foodservice and processing) is a different buying context from consumer or recreational nitrous. CreamTank sells into the professional food and industrial channel and declines recreational or entertainment use.

We do not publish a country-by-country legal digest, and we do not state that a product is permitted or restricted in a named country. This page does not replace advice from your own counsel or the rules that apply where you operate.

MOQ, fulfilment and documents

  • MOQ 1 pallet · no samples · default EU pallet about 504 pcs — see pallet quantity.
  • Dangerous-goods freight as UN1070 Class 2.2 / 5.1, quoted by delivery address. The public site does not list prices or freight amounts.
  • Fulfilment: France (main), Germany and Spain network, plus a UK warehouse for selected SKUs (e.g. FastGas ~670g). See fulfilment, France delivery, Spain delivery, German delivery and UK delivery.
  • EU stock ready — delivery typically within 7 days after payment (truck 1–3 days; transit typically 4–48h). German addresses may ship from other EU warehouses. We do not claim “DE stock ready”.
  • Documents: TPED0036 cylinder conformity (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118) on request for qualified B2B buyers against the SKU inquired. Ask for the current document list. No blanket E942 COA claim. No public certificate PDF pack. See documents and TPED 0036.
