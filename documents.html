Quick answers

What documents can you provide?

TPED0036 cylinder conformity (notified body 0036 / EN ISO 11118 / 2010/35/EU) on request for qualified B2B buyers against the SKU inquired. Ask for the current document list.

Can I download certificates from this website?

No. There is no public certificate download pack. Documents are shared on request after buyer verification.

Do you claim an E942 COA as standard?

No. An E942 COA is not claimed as part of the standard public pack. Food-grade / E942 = positioning / pack marking for professional whipped-cream propellant channels.
